    Road to the Platinum

    Difficulty 4 / 10 · About 200 hours total · One playthrough · No difficulty settings · No mission replay — keep separate saves at the start and end of every chapter · Cheats disable trophies and saving

    ⚠ The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia invites Jason to meditate on the balcony at his house in the morning. Do it before leaving Chapter 1. A meditation counts only after the final zen animation plays.

    1. Step 1 — Story · 60–80 hours

      Play all five chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you travel, because that progress counts toward 100% later. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation when it is offered.

    2. Step 2 — 100% checklist · about 100 hours

      After the credits, work the checklist under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion. Only 70 of the 80 challenges are needed. You do need every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest.

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & cleanup · about 10 hours

      Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands and the side-character final missions, then sweep whatever is left — cards, WAiNK scans, stunts and the last few challenges.

    Grades follow PowerPyx: 6 Silver including Maximum Withdrawal. Push Square counts Maximum Withdrawal as Bronze, giving 5 Silver and 47 Bronze.
